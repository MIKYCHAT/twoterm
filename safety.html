02 • Account security

Protect your account

  • Use a unique password and never share it with moderators.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication if supported by your sign-in provider.
  • Never share one-time login codes.
  • Be cautious of links, fake support accounts, and requests to pay to unlock earnings.
  • Sign out on shared devices and review your active sessions where available.

Security controls must also be enforced by the authentication provider and backend.

Community standards

Community guidelines

Everyone using TWOTERM is expected to follow these rules. Apply them consistently and consider context when reviewing reports.

1. Respect people

Do not threaten, stalk, bully, target, or repeatedly harass people. Do not encourage violence or share private information without permission.

2. No scams or deceptive behaviour

Do not impersonate others, run fraudulent offers, manipulate engagement, steal accounts, or mislead people about payments, gifts, jobs, or earnings.

3. No harmful or unlawful content

Do not post content that exploits people, promotes serious harm, or violates applicable law. Sexual exploitation of minors is prohibited. Never use TWOTERM to facilitate abuse.

4. Respect intellectual property

Only upload or reuse videos, music, images, and other material when you have the rights or permission required. Use the copyright complaint process for suspected infringement.

5. Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's confidential information, private messages, personal documents, or location details to target them.

6. Use LIVE and interactive features responsibly

Do not use LIVE sessions, comments, gifts, polls, or messages to threaten people, defraud viewers, or evade safety measures.

7. Age and sensitive content

TWOTERM's intended platform audience is adults aged 18 and above. Features must use reliable age and eligibility checks where required. Do not assume a simple checkbox verifies someone's age.

8. Enforcement and fairness

Depending on severity and applicable law, actions may include content removal, warnings, feature limits, temporary suspension, or account termination. Users should receive an explanation and an appeal route where eligible.

These are proposed platform rules, not legal advice. Publish a reviewed, final policy before opening the platform to the public.

Copyright and identity

Copyright complaints and impersonation

Copyright: A production workflow should collect the affected work, the allegedly infringing URL, the complainant's required contact information, declarations required by applicable law, and a secure way to submit supporting evidence. Access rule: In production, only authorized moderators should be able to review reports or take action. Verify moderator roles on the server and enforce access through Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) and secure backend functions.

Production moderation requirements

  • Authenticated moderator roles and least-privilege permissions.
  • Private report queue with assignment, timestamps, and review status.
  • Documented reasons for removals, warnings, and restrictions.
  • Appeal handling with independent review where practical.
  • Audit records for administrative actions, protected from unauthorized changes.
  • Rate limits, spam controls, suspicious-activity monitoring, and abuse escalation.
Platform policies

Privacy policy

Draft summary: TWOTERM should explain what account details, profile information, videos, comments, messages, device/security events, and payment-related records it collects; why each category is needed; how long it is retained; who receives it; and how users can exercise their rights.

Terms of service

Draft summary: Terms should explain eligibility, account responsibilities, prohibited behaviour, content ownership and licences, moderation powers, LIVE and gift rules, creator earnings eligibility, payment conditions, suspensions, appeals, account closure, and how updates are communicated.

Creator earnings must not be described as guaranteed. Payment and withdrawal features should have clear eligibility, fees, fraud checks, dispute procedures, and applicable legal disclosures.

These summaries are not a substitute for complete legal documents. Before public launch, publish final policy pages that reflect TWOTERM's real data practices and obtain appropriate legal review for the countries where the platform operates.

Security foundation

Required backend protections

  • Supabase RLS: users should access only records they are permitted to access.
  • Server authorization: check moderator permissions and ownership on every privileged action.
  • Rate limiting: limit report spam, login attempts, messages, and sensitive API requests.
  • Audit logging: record relevant security and moderation actions without exposing secrets.
  • Secure secrets: never put service-role keys, LiveKit API secrets, or payment-provider secrets in browser code.
  • Data lifecycle: implement verified export and deletion workflows with documented retention rules.
  • Abuse detection: monitor suspicious activity and provide a human review route for consequential decisions.

A visible setting, hidden button, or browser-side check is not a security boundary. The database and server must enforce permissions.
