Required revenue transparency

  • Show gross revenue and any deductions separately.
  • Explain the commission rate before the transaction.
  • Disclose processing fees, refunds, chargebacks, and taxes where applicable.
  • Show creator earnings, payout eligibility, and payment status.
  • Keep financial records and restrict access to authorized staff.
  • Never show simulated revenue as real income.

Launch checklist

Before real ads or paid subscriptions launch, the team should implement and test:

  • Secure campaign and advertiser database tables.
  • Server-side authorization and row-level security.
  • Real ad-serving and valid event tracking.
  • Payment provider integration and verified transactions.
  • Advertiser verification and ad moderation.
  • Refunds, disputes, and financial reconciliation.
  • Published advertising and commission policies.
  • Privacy, age restrictions, and applicable legal compliance.
