Privacy, fairness and safety requirements

  • Let creators review AI-assisted captions before publication.
  • Show original text alongside translated content when practical.
  • Provide user controls for recommendations and personalization.
  • Use licensed audio and respect voice and likeness consent.
  • Keep moderation decisions reviewable and provide an appeal route.
  • Use authenticity labels only when their criteria are clear and supported.
  • Offer accessible captions and test with users who rely on them.
  • Keep experiments limited, measurable, and reversible.
