Financial security

  • Daraja consumer secrets and passkeys stay on the server.
  • Verify provider callbacks and reconcile transaction references.
  • Use idempotency so duplicate callbacks cannot duplicate credits.
  • Record refunds, reversals, and withdrawals in an auditable ledger.
  • Apply transaction limits, access controls, and fraud monitoring.
